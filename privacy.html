Privacy Policy

Music Videos · Last updated October 12, 2026

Music Videos is a personal desktop application used to prepare and upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channels. It is not a commercial service, it is not distributed to the public, and it has no users other than the operator of the application.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests access to the YouTube channels of the signed-in Google account solely to upload videos and manage their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and playlists. The app does not access contacts, email, files, or any other Google account data.

How information is used

Google account access is used for one purpose only: publishing the operator's own content to the operator's own YouTube channels. The app does not use this access for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to uploading and managing those videos.

Storage

OAuth tokens issued by Google are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are never transmitted anywhere other than to Google's APIs. The app has no backend server and keeps no database of user information.

Sharing

No information obtained through Google APIs is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Data is transferred only to Google itself, in the course of calling the YouTube API.

Google API Services compliance

Music Videos' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings. Deleting the locally stored token file likewise removes the app's access on that computer.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
